# Europe Connect Pro

I want to build an email a leads b2b email and phone number like  Apollo.io | Find Leads & Close More Deals to crack the code that converts prospects into clients. Get started today with a single click. No credit card...

find accurate and verified emails or phone numbers for free. But mainly focus on European domains then other countries 



Europe

Bulgaria – abv.bg

Germany – gmx.de, web.de

France – orange.fr, wanadoo.fr, free.fr

Italy – libero.it, virgilio.it

Spain – terra.es, ya.com (local legacy ISPs)

Netherlands – planet.nl, kpnmail.nl

Poland – wp.pl, o2.pl, interia.pl

Russia – mail.ru, yandex.ru

Sweden – telia.com (ISP-based)

Austria – gmx.at, aon.at

Czech Republic – seznam.cz, centrum.cz

Romania – local ISP variants, sometimes yahoo.ro in earlier years

Ukraine – typical ISP domains, varied local providers exist

Asia

Hong Kong – netvigator.com, hkbnet.net

Japan – mobile and carrier domains like docomo.ne.jp, softbank.ne.jp

China – qq.com, 163.com, 126.com

India – rediffmail.com, various ISP mail domains (many users still prefer national carriers)

South Korea – naver.com, hanmail.net

Singapore – singnet.com.sg

Philippines – yahoo.com.ph (local Yahoo variant), ISP variants

Malaysia – ISP domains such as tm.net.my

Thailand – local ISP/portal domains and international ones

Indonesia – telkom.net.id, plasa.com

Vietnam – viettel.vn, vnn.vn

Africa

South Africa – iafrica.com, vodamail.co.za (local ISP)

Egypt – local telecom provider domains, usage of global providers also high

Nigeria – some local ISP/telecom mail domains, widespread use of gmail.com, yahoo.com

Kenya – local ISP domains available, but global ones dominate

Americas

Brazil – uol.com.br, bol.com.br (national portals)

Argentina – local ISP or portal domains along with hotmail.com.ar variants

Mexico – ISP domains plus use of hotmail.com.mx, local services

Canada – some local ISP mail domains (e.g., sympatico.ca) though global providers are common

United States – historically AOL/Comcast/att.net, but now largely global providers

Oceania

New Zealand – local ISP and portal domains like sparks.nz

Australia – bigpond.com, optusnet.com.au
